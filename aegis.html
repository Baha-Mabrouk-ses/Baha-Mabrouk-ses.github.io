01 // ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM DESIGN

Unified Edge-to-Cloud Video Intelligence & Command Center

MDI AI Detection (Aegis) transforms passive multi-camera RTSP/PTZ video networks into an active, searchable security intelligence engine. Designed for zero-latency situational awareness, the platform pairs hardware-accelerated GPU inference with granular per-camera confidence tuning, automated license plate OCR, and real-time edge node health monitoring.
